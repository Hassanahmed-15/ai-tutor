# Asset and research credits

- The locally bundled character models are `Michelle.glb` and `readyplayer.me.glb` from the [official Three.js example asset collection](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf). Three.js is distributed under the MIT license; asset provenance remains with the upstream example collection.
- Campus geometry, furniture, materials, signs, and canvas textures are original procedural assets created for this module.
- Accessibility decisions were informed by [W3C XR Accessibility User Requirements](https://www.w3.org/TR/xaur/) and [CAST Universal Design for Learning Guidelines](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/representation/).
- Presence patterns were informed by [Gather's spatial audio and video overview](https://support.gather.town/articles/4624155403-overview-of-spatial-audio-video).
