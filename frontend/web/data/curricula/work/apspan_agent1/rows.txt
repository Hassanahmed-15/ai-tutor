#Skill Category 1: Interpretive Communication
=1.A Recognize explicit meaning
1.A|Recognizing explicit meaning|Recognize explicit meaning in written texts, audio and data visualizations.
1.A.1|Recognizing explicit meaning|Pick out supporting or relevant details in a source.
1.A.2|Recognizing explicit meaning|Identify data points, patterns and trends in charts and graphs.
=1.B Interpret meaning
1.B|Interpreting meaning|Interpret the meaning of written, audio and visual sources.
1.B.1|Interpreting meaning|Determine a source's main idea, theme or purpose.
1.B.2|Interpreting meaning|Determine a source's intended audience.
1.B.3|Interpreting meaning|Determine point of view, perspective, tone or attitude.
1.B.4|Interpreting meaning|Identify organizing and rhetorical structures and strategies.
1.B.5|Interpreting meaning|Work out meanings of words and phrases from textual cues.
=1.C Synthesize and infer meaning
1.C|Synthesizing and inferring|Synthesize and infer meaning across sources.
1.C.1|Synthesizing and inferring|Connect information within or across sources.
1.C.2|Synthesizing and inferring|Synthesize meaning to draw logical conclusions.
1.C.3|Synthesizing and inferring|Infer implied meaning from context.
#Skill Category 2: Interpersonal and Presentational Communication
=2.A Use language that fits purpose and context
2.A|Fitting language to purpose|Use language that suits the communicative purpose and context.
2.A.1|Fitting language to purpose|Use a variety of grammatical structures.
2.A.2|Fitting language to purpose|Use a variety of vocabulary.
2.A.3|Fitting language to purpose|Use idiomatic and culturally relevant expressions.
2.A.4|Fitting language to purpose|Choose a register suited to the audience and situation.
=2.B Make communication comprehensible
2.B|Being understood|Make speech and writing comprehensible to the intended audience.
2.B.1|Being understood|Use circumlocution and paraphrasing as communication strategies.
2.B.2|Being understood|Monitor speech, spot errors and self-correct.
2.B.3|Being understood|Pronounce clearly enough for varied Spanish speakers.
2.B.4|Being understood|Use a suitable speaking rate and intonation.
2.B.5|Being understood|Follow written conventions such as capitalization, spelling, accents and punctuation.
=2.C Share ideas, information, and opinions
2.C|Sharing ideas and opinions|Share ideas, information and opinions on familiar and researched topics.
2.C.1|Sharing ideas and opinions|Express ideas, preferences and opinions.
2.C.2|Researching and citing|Use research strategies to gather information and evidence.
2.C.3|Sharing ideas and opinions|Give examples to advise, inform, or explain opinions and emotions.
2.C.4|Researching and citing|Integrate information from several sources to support a position.
2.C.5|Researching and citing|Reference and cite sources appropriately.
=2.D Apply organizational and rhetorical strategies
2.D|Organizing and connecting ideas|Apply organizational and rhetorical strategies.
2.D.1|Organizing and connecting ideas|Apply an appropriate organizational structure.
2.D.2|Organizing and connecting ideas|Use cohesive devices such as relative pronouns, referents, conjunctions and transitions.

3.A|Cultural connections|Make connections within and across cultures.
3.A.1|Cultural connections|Describe cultural products, practices and perspectives.

3.A.3|Cultural connections|Reflect on the significance of cultural products, practices and perspectives.
