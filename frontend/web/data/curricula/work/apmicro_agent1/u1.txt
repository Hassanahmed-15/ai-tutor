=1.4 Comparative Advantage and Trade
MKT-2.A|Comparative advantage|Define and determine absolute and comparative advantage from PPCs or tables.
MKT-2.A.1|Comparative advantage|Define absolute advantage as producing more with the same resources.
MKT-2.A.2|Comparative advantage|Define comparative advantage as producing at a lower opportunity cost.
MKT-2.B|Gains from trade|Explain gains from specialization and calculate mutually beneficial terms of trade.
MKT-2.B.1|Gains from trade|Explain that specializing by comparative advantage lets consumption go beyond the PPC.
MKT-2.B.2|Gains from trade|Explain that opportunity costs set the range of terms of trade that benefit both sides.
