G|8
U|The Number System
T|Know that there are numbers that are not rational, and approximate them by rational numbers
S|Irrational numbers
8.NS|Know irrational numbers, that every number has a decimal expansion, and convert repeating decimals to fractions
-|Compare and locate irrational numbers using rational approximations and estimate expressions such as pi squared
U|Expressions and Equations
T|Work with radicals and integer exponents
S|Integer exponents and roots
8.EE|Apply properties of integer exponents to make equivalent expressions
-|Use square and cube root symbols to solve x squared = p and x cubed = p, and know the square root of 2 is irrational
S|Scientific notation
-|Estimate very large or small quantities with a single digit times a power of 10
-|Compute with scientific notation and choose sensible units
T|Understand the connections between proportional relationships, lines, and linear equations
S|Slope and lines
-|Graph proportional relationships with unit rate as slope and compare them in different representations
-|Use similar triangles to show slope is constant and derive y = mx and y = mx + b
T|Analyze and solve linear equations and pairs of simultaneous linear equations
S|Linear equations in one variable
-|Show equations with one, infinitely many or no solutions by transforming to simpler forms
-|Solve linear equations with rational coefficients, expanding and collecting like terms
S|Systems of linear equations
-|Understand solutions of a system as intersections of graphs
-|Solve systems algebraically, estimate by graphing and solve simple cases by inspection
-|Solve real-world problems leading to two linear equations
U|Functions
T|Define, evaluate, and compare functions
S|What a function is
8.F|Understand a function as a rule giving each input exactly one output
-|Compare two functions in different forms: algebraic, graphical, table or verbal
-|Interpret y = mx + b as a linear function and give examples of nonlinear functions
T|Use functions to model relationships between quantities
S|Modeling with linear functions
-|Build a linear function, finding rate of change and initial value from a description, two points, a table or a graph
-|Describe a graph qualitatively and sketch a graph from a verbal description
U|Geometry
T|Understand congruence and similarity using physical models, transparencies, or geometry software
S|Transformations and congruence
8.G|Verify experimentally the properties of rotations, reflections and translations
-|Define congruence through rotations, reflections and translations and describe a sequence
S|Dilations and similarity
-|Describe the effect of dilations, translations, rotations and reflections using coordinates
-|Define similarity through transformations including dilations and describe a sequence
-|Use informal arguments for triangle angle sum, exterior angles, parallel lines with a transversal, and angle-angle similarity
T|Understand and apply the Pythagorean Theorem
S|Pythagorean Theorem
-|Explain a proof of the Pythagorean Theorem and its converse
-|Use it to find unknown sides of right triangles in 2D and 3D
-|Use it to find distance between two points in a coordinate system
T|Solve real-world and mathematical problems involving volume of cylinders, cones, and spheres
S|Volumes of round solids
-|Know and use volume formulas for cones, cylinders and spheres
U|Statistics and Probability
T|Investigate patterns of association in bivariate data
S|Scatter plots and linear models
8.SP|Construct and interpret scatter plots and describe clustering, outliers, and positive, negative, linear or nonlinear association
-|Informally fit a straight line to linear scatter plots and judge fit
-|Use a linear model's equation to solve problems, interpreting slope and intercept
S|Two-way tables
-|Build and interpret two-way tables for two categorical variables and use relative frequencies to judge association
